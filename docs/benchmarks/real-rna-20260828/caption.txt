RustScenic 0.5.0 RNA GRN performance on real 10x E18 mouse-brain data. A: six cell-count observations with 2,095 selected genes, 256 TFs, 16 CPU cores and early stopping; the 1,306,127-cell GRN took 46m42s, with 4.28 GB process peak in the measured run. Separate input preparation took 7m18s and peaked at 71.5 GB. B: matched 20,000-cell comparisons on two IFB nodes, showing 2.04× and 3.33× speedups over arboreto 0.1.6. C: the Intel-node comparison used 188.9 versus 995.5 MB physical memory (PSS), approximately 81% less. Each timing is one observation, not a repeated-run mean. Timings exclude loading and reference dense conversion. The figure does not measure a complete SCENIC+ workflow or establish biological equivalence/superiority. Exact values and the archived report accompany the figure.
