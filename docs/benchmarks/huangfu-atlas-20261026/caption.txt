Measured runtime and memory scaling on 100,000, 500,000 and 1,306,127 real 10x E18 mouse-brain cells, using the same ordered 2,095 genes, 256 TFs, 16 CPUs, host and allocation. Points show observed runs; lines connect medians at measured cell counts. The 100k and 500k sizes each have one matched seed (777); 1.3M has three (777, 778, 779), with its observed range shown by a vertical segment, not a confidence interval. Endpoint labels are medians. RustScenic 0.5.0 has 3.06x faster matrix-to-network GRN analysis and 78.7% lower peak sampled process-tree analysis PSS than sparse Dask arboreto 0.1.6 at 1.3M. Against sparse multiprocessing arboreto, the separate result is 2.94x faster and 54.5% lower PSS. Preparation/loading are excluded. Network rankings differ; biological equivalence is not established. Scaling observations and source hashes are in scaling-measurements.csv and scaling-provenance.json; the original nine 1.3M measurements remain unchanged in measurements.csv. make_figure.py rebuilds the light/dark and desktop/mobile graphics.
