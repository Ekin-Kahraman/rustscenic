RustScenic on 1,306,127 real 10x E18 mouse-brain cells, with 2,095 selected genes, 256 TFs and 16 CPUs. Bars show medians and points show the three matched seeds (777, 778, 779). RustScenic 0.5.0 has 3.06x faster matrix-to-network GRN analysis and 78.7% lower peak sampled process-tree analysis PSS than sparse Dask arboreto 0.1.6. Against sparse multiprocessing arboreto, the separate result is 2.94x faster and 54.5% lower PSS. Preparation/loading are excluded. Network rankings differ; biological equivalence is not established.
